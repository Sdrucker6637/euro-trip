Getting Around - Quick Guide

Between countries

Your Eurail pass covers most inter-city legs - just show it on board, no ticket needed. Four legs need a separate paid reservation regardless of the pass: Amsterdam→Paris (Eurostar), Paris→Basel (TGV), and Vienna→Rome (Nightjet sleeper). Book those as early as you can - they have limited seats/berths. Everything else (Basel→Interlaken, Interlaken→Zurich, Zurich→Vienna, Vienna→Budapest) is hop-on with no booking.

Inside each city

Amsterdam & Rome & Budapest: tap your card or Apple Pay directly on trams/metro/buses - no ticket needed.

Paris: no direct tap yet - add a Navigo Easy pass to Apple Wallet instead.

Vienna: contactless isn't confirmed - buy through the WienMobil app to be safe.

Switzerland (Interlaken): no gates at all - it's honor-based. Use the SBB Mobile app (EasyRide) or your Eurail pass.

Apps worth having

Eurostar, SBB Mobile, ÖBB, Nightjet, NS International, WienMobil, BudapestGO, and Moovit/Citymapper for general city navigation.
